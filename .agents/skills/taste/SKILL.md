# Design Taste & Art Direction

1. **Aesthetic Tone & Palette**:
   - AstraOS Astral Meridian palette:
     - Void Background: `#05070d`
     - Surface / Card: `#0b1220`
     - Borders: `#1e3a5f`
     - Primary Accent: Cyan `#5ee6f2`
     - Secondary Accent: Solar Gold `#d6b36a` / `#e6a45a`
     - Text Starlight: `#e8edf7`
     - Text Muted: `#8e9ea9` / `#64748b`

2. **Terminal Micro-Craft**:
   - Clean, purposeful ASCII/Unicode framing (`round` borders, `▌` left indicators, `✦` brand star).
   - Zero empty voids: layouts must expand to fill terminal bounds naturally.
   - Textual widgets must have balanced margins and clear vertical pacing.
